HALOEVE

THE SLEEPY HALLOWEEN PRINCESS // PROFESSIONAL TRICKSTER
🎃 ✦ ☾
ONLY SEEN ON HALLOWEEN // 006 ♡

♡ About Haloeve

Haloeve is a pretty little princess who only appears during Halloween. She spends most of her time hidden deep inside an enchanted forest, sleeping away until October finally arrives.

But don't let her sleepy little face fool you. Haloeve is a mischievous trickster who LOVES causing trouble. Whenever she and her forest friends come out to play, chaos is guaranteed.

She enjoys playing tricks on everyone she meets, stealing little treasures, and disappearing before anyone can catch her.

After causing complete havoc? She goes right back to sleep like nothing happened.

✦ Her Personality

  • Extremely mischievous
  • Always sleepy
  • Loves playing tricks on strangers
  • Thinks she's royalty (because she is)
  • Causes chaos with her forest friends
  • Mysterious and hard to find
  • Only appears around Halloween
☾ HALOEVE'S ROYAL BOOK OF TRICKS ☾

🎃 The Vanishing Pumpkin

Makes everyone's pumpkins disappear, then puts them back in the strangest places.

🌙 Midnight Mischief

Sneaks through the forest rearranging everyone's belongings while they sleep.

✨ The Princess's Curse

Turns someone's next three steps into the silliest little dance.

💤 The Royal Nap

Pretends to fall asleep so nobody suspects she's planning her next prank.

"A princess needs her beauty sleep... after ruining everyone's day."
