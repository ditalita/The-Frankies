♡ About Pollo

Pollo is a very anxious little chicken who worries about absolutely EVERYTHING. A leaf falling? Terrifying. Someone sneezing? The end of the world. A weird noise outside? He's already planning his escape.

He's constantly convinced something terrible is about to happen, even when everything is perfectly fine.

And unfortunately, whenever Pollo gets nervous, he has to poop. Which is pretty much all the time.

✿ His Personality

  • Extremely anxious
  • Overthinks absolutely everything
  • Easily startled
  • Always expects the worst
  • Runs away from his own shadow
  • Has a very nervous stomach
  • Sweet when he isn't panicking
