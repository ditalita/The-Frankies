♡ About Mumad

Mumad is a very peaceful panda who believes that life is better when everyone gets along. He's deeply into meditation and always tries to stay calm, no matter what's happening.

He's a pacifist who avoids fighting and arguments whenever possible. Instead of getting angry, Mumad would rather sit down, talk things out, and make a new friend.

He's thoughtful, patient, and always welcoming to anyone who needs a little peace.
