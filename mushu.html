♡ About Mushu

Mushu is a highly trained kung fu master who has earned not one, not two, but THREE black belts! Despite his adorable appearance, he takes martial arts very seriously.

He has spent years mastering his skills through patience, dedication, and endless practice. Mushu believes true strength comes from both the mind and body.

✿ His Personality

  • Extremely disciplined
  • Wise beyond his years
  • Calm and focused
  • A master of self-control
  • Patient with his students
  • Believes in balance

✦ The Master's Wisdom

"A true master knows
when not to fight."
