♡ About Carrot

Carrot is a very ditzy little bunny who only has one thing on her mind: FOOD!

She's a major glutton and will eat just about anything she can get her paws on. She's constantly thinking about her next meal, even when she's already eating something.

Carrot isn't exactly the brightest bunny in the bunch. She gets distracted easily, forgets what she's doing, and sometimes walks into a room just to forget why she went there.

But somehow, she never forgets where the snacks are hidden.

She's a little clueless, extremely hungry, and completely lovable. ♡

✦ Her Personality

  • Extremely gluttonous
  • Ditzy and scatterbrained
  • Easily distracted, especially by food
  • Goofy and unintentionally funny
  • Always hungry
  • Sweet but a little clueless
