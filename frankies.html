♡ About Scare

Scare is a shy and reclusive little fox who also happens to be the very first Frankie I ever made!

She got the scar on her stomach after getting caught in a fox trap. Ever since then, she's been scared of humans and the outside world, so she prefers to stay hidden somewhere safe.

She only comes out during October, when spooky season makes her feel a little braver. She might look scary, but she's really a sweet little fox who's been through a lot. ♡
